AG Farmer

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Disclosure

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 1.0

Welcome to AG Farmer (Package: com.agfarmer.app). AG Farmer is an AI-powered agricultural advisory assistant application engineered to assist farmers, cultivators, and agrarian specialists with modern crop tracking, pest identification, daily field schedules, and localized farm intelligence.

Agricultural Advisory Notice: AG Farmer provides advisory insights generated by agronomic algorithms and artificial intelligence. All recommendations regarding fertilizer dosages, pesticide sprays, irrigation timings, and crop schedules are advisory in nature. Farmers should always account for local soil reports, crop growth stages, chemical manufacturer safety instructions, and consult local Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK) or agricultural extension officers before chemical applications.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization and collect only the data strictly necessary to deliver personalized farming guidance:

  • Account & Profile Data: Name, email address, phone number, age (18+), and state/region entered during email sign-up or Google One-Tap Sign-In.
  • Crop Tracking & Field Records: User-selected crops (e.g., Garlic/लहसुन, Opium Poppy/अफीम, Wheat, Mustard, Onion, Chilli), sowing dates, field area (bigha/acres), and daily activity logs.
  • AI Chat & Voice Inquiries: Questions, crop symptom descriptions, and voice transcriptions submitted to the AG Farmer AI assistant to provide tailored agronomic responses.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Metrics: Anonymous crash reports, device model, and OS version via Firebase Crashlytics to maintain zero-lag operation across ₹5,000 to ₹100,000+ devices.

2. Sensitive Permissions & Hardware Access

In adherence to Google Play Store's Permission Minimization Guidelines:

  • No Background Location: We do NOT access your background GPS location.
  • No Contact or SMS Access: AG Farmer never requests read/write access to your contacts or SMS.
  • Network Access Only: The app utilizes standard INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE solely to communicate with Google Firebase encrypted backends.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

All user data is managed by the AG Farmer backend. यह सारा डेटा AG Farmer का बैकएंड मैनेज करता है।

We do NOT sell, monetize, rent, or trade your personal or agricultural data to advertising brokers or commercial third parties.

4. Google Play Generative AI Policy Compliance

AG Farmer complies with all Google Play Generative AI rules:

  • Safety Filters: AI responses are filtered against hate speech, hazardous instructions, and illicit chemical handling.
  • In-App User Reporting: Every AI response features a visible Flag / Report button. Users can immediately report inaccurate, harmful, or inappropriate content.
  • Continuous Review: Reported answers are routed to agricultural specialists for prompt review and model tuning.

5. User Data Rights & Account Deletion

Farmers have absolute ownership over their data:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: Open the AG Farmer app → Go to Profile → Scroll to Danger Zone → Tap "Delete Account & Data". Your entire account, Firebase database nodes, and crop tracks are purged permanently.
  • Web-Based Deletion: If you have already uninstalled the app, you can submit a deletion request via our Account Deletion Portal or email us directly at ag.farmer.mail@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

AG Farmer is intended exclusively for agricultural operators and farmers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors under 13. If such information is discovered, it is immediately deleted.

7. Data Security Standards

All communication between the AG Farmer mobile application and our backend is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Firebase security rules prevent unauthorized third parties from accessing your farm data.
