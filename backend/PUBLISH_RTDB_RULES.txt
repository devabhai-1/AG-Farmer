PASTE THIS IN FIREBASE CONSOLE (required once so profiles save):

1. Open:
https://console.firebase.google.com/project/ag-farmer1/database/ag-farmer1-default-rtdb/rules

2. Replace ALL rules with the contents of:
   backend/firebase/database.rules.json

3. Click Publish

4. Then create account / login again in the app.

After this, every account saves to:
  ag_farmer/users/{uid}/profile
  ag_farmer/email_users/{email}/profile
  (includes IP, createdAt, name, phone, address, age, plan, …)
