Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Data Safety Compliant Permanent Deletion Instructions

⚠ Irreversible Action

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the AG Farmer app installed on your phone, you can permanently erase your account and all associated farm records in seconds with real-time Firebase cleanup:

  1. Open the AG Farmer mobile app.
  2. Tap the Profile (प्रोफाइल) icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the red Danger Zone (खतरे का क्षेत्र) section.
  4. Tap "Delete Account & Data" (अकाउंट और सारा डेटा डिलीट करें).
  5. Confirm the prompt. Your user account, crop tracking nodes, field notes, and chat logs are immediately and permanently erased from Google Firebase servers.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (App Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a verified deletion request below. Our automated backend processes requests within 48 business hours:

What Data is Deleted?

  • Account Profile: Name, phone number, email address, and Firebase Auth UID.
  • Field Data: All crop tracks, custom schedules, sowing logs, and crop yield notes.
  • Chat History: All AI conversation history and custom query tokens.

Note: Any active Google Play subscriptions must be canceled directly in Google Play Store settings to prevent Google from recurring charges.
